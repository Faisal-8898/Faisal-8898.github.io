Research

Edge intelligence for IoT

PAUMIoT

A middleware that accepts MQTT, CoAP and HTTP on a single port and identifies the protocol from the first bytes of each flow. On the device, an ESP32 client uses a LinUCB contextual bandit to choose a protocol, while a safety shield falls back to a safe option when the battery or signal is low. Each decision takes under 50 µs on the ESP32.

My part: the ESP32 client, the safety shield and the hardware testbed. IEEE DCOSS-IoT 2026.

IoT security measurement

Passive attacker attribution

I ran four honeypots on one cloud server for 112 days and collected about 4.0 million events from 29,052 IP addresses. The study asks whether attackers can be grouped by passive signals, such as SSH client fingerprints, passwords and command patterns, which stay the same even when attackers change their IP addresses.

Independent study. Journal paper in preparation.

Explainable machine learning

Interpretable prediction

In two group projects, we paired standard models with statistical tests and explanations. For early diabetes prediction, Random Forest reached 96.79% accuracy on 520 patient records, and LIME showed which symptoms drove each prediction. A second study combines ARIMA-LSTM forecasting with SHAP and LIME for retail inventory and pricing.

IEEE BECITHCON 2025. Engineering Reports, under review.

Networks and emulation

SEFIoT and CCN freshness

SEFIoT is a lightweight Python emulator that generates sensor traffic modelled on Dhaka, and it ran 20,000 sensors in about 0.16 GB of memory. In a separate study, I extended The ONE simulator with a CCN layer to measure how often cached content in delay-tolerant networks is already out of date.

IEEE QPAIN 2026. NSysS 2026, submitted.
